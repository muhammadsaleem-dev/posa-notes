Proven Exam Preparation Strategy

1. Active Retrieval Practice

Click "Collapse All for Self-Testing" on the mock exam page. Read the question, write your answer on paper or recall it from memory, and then click to reveal the verified answer. Testing yourself before reading cements memories 300% more effectively than passive re-reading.

2. Study the "Professor Gotchas"

Pay close attention to the yellow ⚠️ Gotcha Boxes. These reflect common point deduction traps on university exams (e.g., confusing Strategy with State, forgetting that Blackboard Knowledge Sources never talk directly to each other, or calculating package Instability inversely).
