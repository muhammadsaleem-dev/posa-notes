Unit 1 · Behavioral

Strategy Pattern

The SimUDuck evolution: why inheritance breaks when requirements change. Program to interfaces, favor composition over inheritance, and dynamic runtime sorters.

Unit 2A · Behavioral

Template Method

Abstracting common algorithmic skeletons into invariant superclass methods. Hollywood Principle (“Don’t call us, we’ll call you!”), hooks, and inheritance vs composition tradeoffs.

Explore Unit 2A →
Unit 2B · Behavioral

Command Pattern

Encapsulating requests as first-class objects. Programmable remote controls, the Null Object Pattern (NoCommand), dependency injection, and reversible LIFO undo stacks.

Explore Unit 2B →
Unit 3A & 3B · Structural & Behavioral

Composite & Iterator

Walking composite structures without exposing internal arrays. Representing nested trees uniformly and evaluating arithmetic expression trees.

Unit 6 · Principles

SOLID & Design Smells

The 7 fatal design smells (Rigidity, Fragility, Viscosity, Immobility). The 5 SOLID principles (SRP, OCP, LSP, ISP, DIP) and why Square is NOT a Rectangle in OO.

Explore Unit 6 →
Unit 7 · Principles & Metrics

Packaging & Metrics

Package Cohesion (REP, CRP, CCP) and Coupling (ADP, SDP, SAP). Mathematical metrics: Instability ($I$), Abstractness ($A$), and Normalized Distance ($D$) from the Main Sequence.

Explore Unit 7 →
Unit 12 · Architecture

Pipes & Filters & Blackboard

Macro-level streaming pipelines versus centralized opportunistic AI blackboards for non-deterministic speech and sensor processing. Apache hooks case study.

Explore Unit 12 →
Unit 4 · Architecture Case Study

Case Study: JUnit Cook's Tour

How Erich Gamma and Kent Beck composed Composite (TestSuite), Command (TestCase), and Template Method (runBare) into an elegant extensible testing framework.
