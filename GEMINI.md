# POSA Course & Exam Preparation Rules

These rules and preferences apply to all work, modifications, and generation of content across this project.

---

## 1. Exam Naming & Separation
- **Mock Exam 1 (`mock-1.html`)**: Core practice paper consisting of 8 comprehensive exercises (103 Points Total, 90-minute timed pace).
- **Mock Exam 2 (`mock-2.html`)**: Advanced practice paper consisting of 17 deep-dive questions across 4 modules (95 Points Total).
- **Never merge or swap them**: They serve distinct pedagogical roles. Keep them clearly distinguished as **Mock Exam 1** and **Mock Exam 2**.

---

## 2. Formatting & Structure: Keep It Simple
- **Direct Question $\to$ Answer format**:
  - State the question clearly with its exact point badge.
  - State the complete model solution/answer directly underneath.
- **No overcomplication**:
  - Do NOT wrap questions or answers in nested cards, complex accordions, or hidden tabs.
  - Answers must be readily readable and directly accessible.

---

## 3. Neutral Branding & Anonymity
- **Zero Instructor Attribution**:
  - Never include professor/lecturer names, titles, or email addresses anywhere in the pages, headers, or metadata.
- **Neutral Practice Terminology**:
  - Never label materials as "actual exam paper", "authentic Klausur", or "real exam".
  - Strictly use neutral labels: **Mock Exam 1** and **Mock Exam 2**.

## 5. Git Workflow & Commit Hygiene (No Spam Commits)
- **Zero Micro-Commit Spam**: Do NOT commit or push after every small edit or single-file change.
- **Batch Locally**: Keep all intermediate work, tests, and edits local until an entire unit of work, chapter, or milestone is finished and verified.
- **Single Consolidated Push**: Push only when a milestone is completed, grouping related work into one clean, meaningful commit.
